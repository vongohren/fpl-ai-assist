## Analysis

### Method: the GW6 model, blended

The calculation the proposal page shows (`scripts/gw-loop/proposal-page.mjs`), run over every
player in the game from a scratch copy, then blended with the consensus for GW11:

```
xPts per fixture = P(start) × [ 2 + (xG90 × goal_value + xA90 × 3) × attack_mult
                 + P(CS) × cs_value − 0.5 × λ_against (GK/DEF) + 2 × P(DC threshold)
                 + saves (GK) + bonus ]
blended          = 0.7 × calc + 0.3 × consensus    (w 0.3, fixed, 1 of 6 gameweeks)
blended six      = calc six + (blended − calc)      (only GW11 is blended)
```

Each option is the best legal XI and captain of its fifteen on the blended number, re-picked every
week and summed over GW11-16. Every single and every affordable pair of transfers was searched
(budget £1.5m + sells, club limit, minutes gate: 300+ minutes and `a` or `d`). The page's own
squad totals pick one XI on six-week numbers and come out slightly lower (331.7 against 325.4); the
ranking of the options is the same.
