## Learnings

### What went right, by luck rather than process

- **B.Fernandes captaincy returned 23 (46 doubled)** against the field's Haaland at 13
  (26). A **+20 swing** on the armband in a single week, and the entire reason for the
  rank jump. The GW1 decision to captain him was vindicated here, not in GW1.
- **The no-Haaland build gained for the first time.** GW1's log warned that surviving
  week one proved nothing. This is the first week where the exposure actively paid.

### What this week exposed

- **Three squad slots produced nothing and nobody noticed.** Bruno G. (19 min),
  Wilson (45 min) and Muñoz (0 min, autosubbed) contributed 2 points between them from
  £18.6m of squad value. A 93-point week masked it completely. This went unaddressed
  until GW3.
- **A 93-point week is not evidence the squad is healthy.** It was one captain haul
  plus Gibbs-White. Six of the eleven starters returned 2 points or fewer.
- **Rolling the transfer was the right outcome for the wrong reason.** Having 2 FTs in
  GW3 was what made the -4 restructure affordable rather than a -8. But it was rolled
  by inattention, not by plan.
- **No evaluation ran before the deadline.** GW1's log explicitly flagged that *"no
  recurring watcher was scheduled"*. That gap cost nothing this week and would have cost
  real points in a week where an injury needed reacting to.

### Adjustment carried into GW3

Audit every squad slot for minutes, not just points, every single week. Points hide
dead weight when the captain hauls.
