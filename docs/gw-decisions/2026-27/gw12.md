## Analysis

### Method: the GW6 model, blended

The calculation the proposal page shows (`scripts/gw-loop/proposal-page.mjs`), run over every
player in the game from a scratch copy, then blended with the consensus for GW12:

```
xPts per fixture = P(start) × [ 2 + (xG90 × goal_value + xA90 × 3) × attack_mult
                 + P(CS) × cs_value − 0.5 × λ_against (GK/DEF) + 2 × P(DC threshold)
                 + saves (GK) + bonus ]
blended          = 0.7 × calc + 0.3 × consensus    (w 0.3, fixed, 2 of 6 gameweeks)
blended six      = calc six + (blended − calc)      (only GW12 is blended)
```

Each option is the best legal XI and captain of its fifteen on the blended number, re-picked every
week and summed over GW12-17. Every single transfer was searched (budget £1.0m + sell, club limit,
minutes gate: 300+ minutes and `a` or `d`). Raya's number is at his 50% for GW12 and 70% after
(the page's rule for `d`). The page's own totals pick one XI on six-week numbers: **324.0 against
317.4** for keeping the squad.

the best outfield move is worth 1.6. Arteta's "doesn't look too serious" is why Raya is kept and

**Why Verbruggen and not Kinsky.** Kinsky is 0.26 better over six (inside the band) and rising, but
Spurs play at Sunderland, and **Le Fée starts for us** (he takes Mbeumo's place on the 0.3 rule).

keep Kinsky means starting Mbeumo, who fails the falling-starter margin. Verbruggen is **better in
GW12 itself** (+1.84 against +1.50: Brighton at home to Newcastle, the fourth-worst attack), has
started all eleven (990 minutes, 7 clean sheets, 59 points), is the Scout's and Fix's named
replacement (buy 2 of 4 against Kinsky's 1), and 14 of the top 50 own him. Cost of the swap on the
model: 0.26 over six.
